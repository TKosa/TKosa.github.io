New Features

\[ ] title screen implemented as popup (after map is generated/rendered). Game paused until we click start. Popup describes game basics. 

\[ ] Items can be crafted into other items. Items dropped from enemies are Tier 1. The tier of a crafted item is 1 + the tier of the highest ingredient. Crafting is accessible from the menu, with about 9 Tier 1 items.

\[ ] When four small enemies are killed, a medium enemy spawns. The medium enemy drops an item directly into the inventory if space is available.

\[ ] Building upgrades: Right-click gun bullet brings up an upgrade window when it collides with a building.

\[ ] Turrets fire missles at enemies. missles are identical to what user would fire.
