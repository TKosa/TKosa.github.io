# Flight Simulator Game Features

This document outlines the core features of the flight simulator game.

## Core Gameplay

*   **3D Flight Simulation:** Experience the thrill of flying a plane in a 3D environment.
*   **Combat:** Engage in fast-paced aerial combat with enemy planes.
*   **Exploration:** Explore a large, procedurally generated city.
*   **Progression:** Level up your plane and unlock new abilities.

## Player

*   **Plane Control:**
    *   Control the plane's movement using the keyboard (Arrow keys, WASD).
    *   Use the spacebar to control the throttle.
*   **Upgradable Stats:**
    *   Improve your plane's performance by upgrading its stats:
        *   **Health:** Increase your plane's durability.
        *   **Rotation Speed:** Turn faster and more efficiently.
        *   **Max Speed:** Fly faster to outmaneuver your enemies.
        *   **Acceleration:** Reach top speed more quickly.
        *   **Damage:** Increase the damage of your bullets.
        *   **Hitbox Size:** Decrease the size of your plane's hitbox to avoid enemy fire.
        *   **Star Collection Radius:** Collect stars from a greater distance.
        *   **Bullet Size:** Increase the size of your bullets.
        *   **Bullet Lifetime:** Increase the duration your bullets stay on the screen.
        *   **Bullet Range:** Increase the distance your bullets can travel.
*   **Combat:**
    *   Fire bullets to shoot down enemies.
    *   Use special homing bullets that track enemies.

## Enemies

*   **Enemy Types:**
    *   **Red Enemy:** The weakest enemy type.
    *   **Orange Enemy:** A medium-strength enemy that chases the player.
    *   **Black Enemy:** The strongest enemy type, with high health and damage.
*   **Enemy Behavior:**
    *   **Basic Patrol:** Enemies patrol a designated area.
    *   **Chase Player:** Enemies actively chase and attack the player.
    *   **Go to Center:** Enemies fly towards the center of the map.
*   **Enemy Attacks:**
    *   Enemies can shoot at the player.
    *   Enemies can attack and destroy buildings.

## Items & Crafting

*   **Inventory:**
    *   Collect and store items in your inventory.
    *   Use items to gain an advantage in combat.
*   **Item Types:**
    *   **Shield:** Provides temporary protection from enemy fire.
    *   **Bomb:** Creates a large explosion that damages all enemies in a radius.
    *   **Campfire:** Heals the player over time.
    *   **Turret:** Deploys a stationary turret that automatically shoots at enemies.
*   **Crafting:**
    *   Combine items in your inventory to craft new and more powerful items.
    *   Example: Shield + Campfire = Bomb

## World & UI

*   **Procedurally Generated City:**
    *   Explore a unique and ever-changing city with procedurally generated buildings.
*   **Minimap:**
    *   Navigate the world using a minimap that displays the player, enemies, and stars.
*   **UI Elements:**
    *   **Health Bar:** Keep track of your plane's health.
    *   **Star Counter:** See how many stars you've collected.
    *   **Level Display:** Track your current level.
    *   **Inventory Display:** View your items and their cooldowns.
    *   **Stats Overlay:** See your plane's current stats.
    *   **Level-Up Menu:** Choose your upgrades when you level up.
    *   **Enemy HP Display:** See the health of an enemy when you hover over it.
